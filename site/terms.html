What ChainCheck is

ChainCheck displays winter road conditions, chain control levels, weather forecasts, and resort snow reports for the Tahoe area, using data published by Caltrans, the CHP, the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, and the resorts themselves. ChainCheck is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency or any resort.

The important part: verify before you drive. Mountain conditions change faster than any app can follow. Data can be wrong, delayed, or missing, and a summary produced by software (including the AI trip brief) can contain mistakes. ChainCheck never tells you that a road is safe. Before driving, verify conditions with official sources: dial 511 in California or check quickmap.dot.ca.gov. Whether and how to drive is always your decision and your responsibility.
